Economy

Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest economy. Growth held near 5% through the post-pandemic years, inflation returned inside the central bank target band, and exports have exceeded imports every year since 2020.

People

A young, increasingly connected population: a quarter of Indonesians are under 15, literacy is near universal, and life expectancy keeps climbing.

Methodology & sources

Where the numbers come from

All headline figures are published series of BPS — Statistics Indonesia (Badan Pusat Statistik), the national statistics office: national accounts for GDP, the consumer price index for inflation, the National Labour Force Survey (Sakernas) for unemployment, the National Socioeconomic Survey (Susenas) for poverty, and the population census and intercensal estimates for population.

How to read this page

Every figure carries its period — a year, a month, or a survey round — because statistics describe a moment, not a timeless fact. Values are rounded to the precision BPS uses in its own headlines; small differences from other summaries usually come from revisions between release rounds.

About this site

Indonesia in Numbers is an independent summary site. It is not the BPS website and does not speak for BPS; it republishes BPS headline figures with attribution so they are easy to find, read and compare. For official tables and microdata, consult BPS publications directly.
